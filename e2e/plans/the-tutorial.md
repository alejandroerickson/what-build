# The tutorials

The tutorials show a newcomer around a product: the screen darkens around one thing at a time and
a bubble names it. A short welcome (the WhatBuild tutorial) is offered once in the example
organization and once more on the first product of one's own; after it, each of a
product's four pages has a tutorial of its own, which starts by itself the first time the page is
opened, on example content that ships with the web app and replaces the product's own while it
runs. Which have been shown, and whether tutorials are off, is kept on the account. The examples
must never reach an agent. Every step starts at the sign-in page and reaches a product by clicking,
as a person arrives, never by opening a product's address.

## Steps

1. **MCP, as Casey, in the synthetic organization.** List a product's sources, mockups and Story
   pages, and note their counts and titles. *Expect:* the tools list has nothing named for a
   tutorial.
2. **Web, as Casey, from the sign-in page.** Sign in and wait on the screen you land on (the
   overview), clicking nothing. *Expect:* after a moment, a welcome over a darkened screen, "Take
   the WhatBuild tutorial?", "Under a minute: the four pages, and an agent at work.", with Turn off
   tutorials, Not now and Start the tutorial; focus is on Start the tutorial. Press Start the
   tutorial, in light, at 1280 wide or more. *Expect:* the product's Idea, the product's own
   content (no examples), at "1 of 2": Main navigation lit, then with Next the agent stage, most of
   the screen, a chat in the opposite theme to the app's beside a small WhatBuild whose four pages
   fill in as the agent calls `sources_write` (create), `outcomes_write` (create), `mockups_write`
   (publish) and `story_write` (publish); Replay starts it again; its button reads Finish (the MCP
   client of step 1 is an agent connected, so there is no Connect an agent stop). Press Finish.
   *Expect:* it flies into the account button, which pulses, and a toast "Tutorial finished. It
   stays in your account menu."; Idea's own tutorial does not start, however long you wait.
3. **Web, as Casey, Evidence.** Open Evidence from the navigation. *Expect:* after a moment the
   Evidence tutorial, "1 of 3", Evidence lit in the navigation; the product's own content replaced
   by examples marked Tutorial, under the line "Tutorial examples. They disappear when you close
   the tutorial. Agents never see them.", while the navigation's counts stay the product's own.
   Each stop lights the thing its title names and nothing else clickable. At stop 2 the light rests
   on the source's row, not selected, the preview closed, until Next. Press Next. *Expect:* the row
   is selected, the preview opens beside the list on its whiteboard photo, page 2 of 3, and the
   light moves to it. Back. *Expect:* the preview closes and the light is on the row. Close it with
   the close button. *Expect:* the toast "The tutorial is in your account menu." with Show me,
   which opens the account menu with **Take the Evidence tutorial**, "3 stops", first, above
   Settings. Reload, and open Evidence again. *Expect:* nothing starts. Open a source's own screen
   and take the tutorial from the account menu. *Expect:* the list of sources, at its first stop.
4. **Web, as Casey, Idea.** Open Idea from the navigation. *Expect:* the Idea tutorial, "1 of 5".
   Stop 3 lights the More info button alone, the text closed, the bubble's beak on the button, and
   stays so, however long you wait, until Next. Press Next. *Expect:* the button shows its press,
   the text unfolds and the light grows to the card's head and text, stop 4 with the beak still on
   the button. Back. *Expect:* the text folds and the light is back on the button. Forward to stop
   5, then Back. *Expect:* the text open at once. Click the dark. *Expect:* the close button is
   pointed at, and nothing closes. Press Esc. *Expect:* it closes into the account menu.
5. **Web, as Casey, Exploration.** Open Exploration. *Expect:* the Exploration tutorial, "1 of
   6". At the canvas stop press 1, 2, 3, V, H and F, pan and zoom with the mouse. *Expect:* each key
   cap in the bubble turns green with a check; the canvas responds; F hides the panels and the
   canvas takes the window, the light follows it and the bubble sits inside it at the top left,
   clear of the toolbar; F again, or Show panels, brings them back. Go into Focus and press Next,
   then Back, then go into Focus and press Back. *Expect:* the panels back at every other stop. Go
   on to the toolbar stop. *Expect:* the canvas in Canvas view with the panels showing, the toolbar
   lit and unchanged, each control labelled with its name and its key on a leader line (Pan H,
   Interact V, Zoom out −, Zoom in +, Fit the page 2, Fill the width 3, Fit the canvas 1, Previous
   page ←, Next page →, Keyboard shortcuts), no label overlapping another or leaving the window, the
   bubble clear of the bar and the labels, at 1024, 1280 and 1440 wide, in light and in dark.
   Clicking the toolbar does nothing. Go on and switch View to Clickable prototype and back.
   *Expect:* the switch works. With focus still on the switch, press / and F; then in the bubble.
   *Expect:* no search and no focus mode either time. At the toolbar stop at 1100 wide, narrow the
   window to 1024. *Expect:* the light grows with the toolbar onto its second row. Promote canvas to
   Story and Download as ZIP are shown and inert. Finish.
6. **Web, as Casey, turning tutorials off.** Open Story. *Expect:* the Story tutorial, "1 of 3".
   Press **Turn off tutorials**. *Expect:* it closes, with the toast "Tutorials are off. Turn them
   on again in Settings." and Undo; the product's own content is back. Open Settings → Account.
   *Expect:* Tutorials, "Start each page’s tutorial the first time you open it", unchecked. Take the
   Story tutorial from the account menu on Story. *Expect:* it runs, with no Turn off tutorials in
   its foot.
7. **Web, as Casey, in a second browser.** Sign in. *Expect:* no welcome, and no tutorial starts on
   any page. Check Tutorials in Settings → Account. *Expect:* still nothing starts on Idea,
   Evidence, Exploration or Story: each has been shown.
8. **MCP, as Casey.** List the same sources, mockups and Story pages as in step 1. *Expect:* the
   same counts and titles: nothing was added, changed or archived.
9. **Web, as Avery, from the sign-in page.** Wait on the screen you land on, then open the
   synthetic organization's Story. *Expect:* no welcome and no tutorial on either, and no tutorial
   item in the account menu. **Below 1024 wide (1023 × 768), as someone not yet offered it (Sam):**
   no welcome and no item. At 1024 wide, the welcome. With a tutorial up at 1100 wide, narrow the
   window to 900. *Expect:* it closes, with the toast "The tutorial is in your account menu.";
   widened again, the menu offers it.
10. **Web, as a guest of the example organization, in dark.** Sign in with a new address and wait
    on the screen you land on. *Expect:* the welcome there, before any click. Press Not now.
    *Expect:* it flies into the account button, and the toast says the tutorial is in the account
    menu. Open a product of the example. *Expect:* the product's Idea starts its own tutorial (the
    welcome was not offered there), "1 of 6", ending on the "No agent connected" seat, "Connect an
    agent", the tutorial's line in place of the example's banner while it runs. Press Finish, then
    open Evidence. *Expect:* its own, "1 of 4", ending on the same seat. Press Connect an agent. *Expect:* it flies into the account button, Settings opens at
    Agents, and a toast says "Tutorial finished. It stays in your account menu." Everything in the
    bubble, the welcome, the pill and the badges reads in dark. Repeat a few stops at 1024 wide.
    *Expect:* the bubble stays inside the window and never covers what it names.
11. **Web, as the same guest, starting an organization of their own, in light at 1280 wide.**
    First, as Riley, in Instance admin → Guests, set **New organizations a day** to 2 if it is less:
    one place for this guest and one for the new address in step 12. With only 1, step 12 joins the
    waitlist. As the guest, open Exploration in the example and close its tutorial. Then open Story,
    whose tutorial has not been shown yet: Idea's and Evidence's were shown in step 10, so they start
    nothing. Close Story's tutorial with Esc, and while its toast "The tutorial is in your account
    menu." is up, press **Start using WhatBuild** in the demo's banner. *Expect:* the Welcome form
    with no toast, since it has no account menu. Name an organization and a product and press Start
    organization. *Expect:* the new product's Idea, Connect your agent first, and a moment later the
    same welcome as in step 10, "Take the WhatBuild tutorial?", over the darkened screen, before any
    click; no toast from the example. Press Start the tutorial and walk it to Finish: Main
    navigation, Agents, then the seat. Reload, and open Evidence, Exploration and Story. *Expect:* no
    welcome and no tutorial: each page's was shown in the example. `GET /api/v1/me` lists
    `welcome-own` in `tutorial.seen`.
12. **Web, as a new address that never opens the example, at 1280 wide.** Press Start using
    WhatBuild on `/site/` and sign in. *Expect:* the Welcome form, not the waitlist: the second of
    the day's places. Start an organization. *Expect:* the welcome on its Idea. Press Not now and
    reload. *Expect:* no welcome; Idea's own tutorial starts, since the welcome closed on Idea leaves
    it for the next visit. Close it, and open a product of the example. *Expect:* no welcome there
    either.

## Look out for

- Anything of the product's own content showing while a page's tutorial runs, or the examples
  staying after it closes.
- A request for the examples in the browser's network log (the whiteboard photo is the app's own
  file), or any of them in an MCP answer.
- A tutorial left open, half drawn or stuck after a reload, a sign-out, the browser's Back, or a
  guest's waiting page; a tutorial starting by itself a second time, or while tutorials are off.
- Two tutorials one after the other on the same visit to a page (the welcome, then Idea's).
- Focus escaping the bubble with Tab, or not coming back where it was when the tutorial closes.
- A welcome that waits for a click into the product instead of coming on the first screen.
- A welcome offered a third time, or a second time on one's own products; none on the first product
  of one's own after the example.
- A toast about the account menu on a screen that has none, such as the Welcome form.
